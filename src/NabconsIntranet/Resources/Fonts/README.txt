Fonts for the e-Note archival PDF (embedded in NabconsIntranet.exe and in each PDF).

Noto Sans (Regular, Bold) and Noto Sans Devanagari (Regular, Bold), Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors,
licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt). Source: https://github.com/notofonts

Modified versions (permitted by the OFL), made with fontTools pyftsubset to keep the program and PDFs small:
- Noto Sans: limited to Latin, Latin Extended, combining marks, general punctuation, currency signs (incl. U+20B9 ₹),
  letter-like symbols, arrows and the f-ligatures; hinting removed (not used in PDF).
- Noto Sans Devanagari: all glyphs kept; hinting removed.
