# NABCONS Intranet — Quick guide for staff

## My Desk
**My Desk** (top menu) lists everything waiting for you, oldest first:
- requests you must review or sanction, and your requests returned to you;
- administrators: registrations and vertical changes to approve;
- BDU members and LeadViewers: new leads, and your assigned leads that are due;
- your latest notifications.

The number next to *My Desk* counts the items waiting for your action. By default the intranet opens My Desk when you sign in. To change this, go to **My Profile → Preferences**.

## Notifications (the bell)
- The bell at the top shows how many notifications you have not read. Click it, or press **Enter** on it, to see the latest ten.
  - Use the **arrow keys** to move through them and **Esc** to close.
  - Opening a notification takes you to the item and marks it read. *Mark all read* clears them all.
- *View all* opens the **Notifications** page, where you can filter by unread or all, and by module.
- The intranet checks for new notifications about once a minute while the page is open. It does not keep you signed in: you are still signed out after 30 minutes without using the intranet.
- Notifications appear only inside the intranet. Nothing is sent by e-mail or SMS.

## e-Notes (digital note-sheets)
**e-Notes** (top menu) replaces the paper note-sheet ("green sheet") for routine matters.

1. **Write a note.** Click *New e-Note*. Add a subject and category, then write the note. Leave a blank line between paragraphs; they are numbered for you. Attach supporting files: PDFs for reference documents, Word files for draft letters so they stay editable. Tick the declaration and click **Save draft**. A draft is visible only to you, and you can change it freely.
2. **Mark it.** On the note, under *Submit and mark to*, type two or more letters of a colleague's name and choose them from the list. You can use the arrow keys and Enter. Follow the usual office hierarchy: the intranet does not impose one. The note gets its number (for example `NCS/EN/HR/2026-27/0001`) when you submit it.
3. **When a note is with you**, it appears under *My Desk* and *e-Notes → Pending with me*. You can:
   - **Comment** (your remarks are added to the note-sheet, signed with your name, designation and time) and **attach** files.
   - **Mark to** anyone, including someone who has already seen the note, for example to ask an earlier officer for comments. Remarks are required.
   - **Send back** to the person who sent it to you, or **Return to initiator** if the initiator must revise the note.
   - As the competent authority, **Approve** or **Reject**, with your decision or reason.
4. **When it is returned to you** (the initiator): *Revise the note*, then mark it onward again. Every submitted text is kept as a numbered version; nothing is overwritten.
5. **When it is decided**, it comes back to you. Download the **PDF for archival** if you need it for the physical file, then **Close / file** it. You can **withdraw** a note at any time before a decision.

**Who can see a note:**
- you, and the people it was marked to;
- once **approved**, all staff can read it under *Approved notes*, unless you ticked **Confidential** when you created it.

Nothing on a note can be edited or deleted after it is submitted; to correct something, add a comment or a newer attachment.

**Do not** use e-Notes for disciplinary, vigilance or complaint matters. Those stay on the physical file.
