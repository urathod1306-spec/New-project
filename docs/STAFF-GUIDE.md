# NABCONS Intranet — Quick guide for staff

## My Desk
**My Desk** (top menu) lists everything waiting for you, oldest first:
- requests you must review or sanction, and your requests returned to you;
- administrators: registrations and vertical changes to approve;
- BDU members and LeadViewers: new leads, and your assigned leads that are due;
- your latest notifications.

The number next to *My Desk* counts the items waiting for your action. By default the intranet opens My Desk when you sign in. To change this, go to **My Profile → Preferences**.

## Notifications (the bell)
- The bell at the top shows how many notifications you have not read. Click it, or press **Enter** on it, to see the latest ten.
  - Use the **arrow keys** to move through them and **Esc** to close.
  - Opening a notification takes you to the item and marks it read. *Mark all read* clears them all.
- *View all* opens the **Notifications** page, where you can filter by unread or all, and by module.
- The intranet checks for new notifications about once a minute while the page is open. It does not keep you signed in: you are still signed out after 30 minutes without using the intranet.
- Notifications appear only inside the intranet. Nothing is sent by e-mail or SMS.
